HOW IT WORKS

Straightforward from Survey to Completion

1. Tell Us About Your Property

Complete our enquiry form and provide the address, services required and any useful information about the property.
Photographs can also help us understand the scale and access before arranging a visit.

2. Property or Site Survey

For larger homes, commercial properties and complex projects, we may arrange a visit to assess access, surfaces and the work required.

3. Receive a Clear Proposal

We will provide a quotation based on the agreed scope, property requirements and proposed schedule.

4. Your Work Is Scheduled

Once approved, your project is assigned to the appropriate members of our team and booked for an agreed date.

5. Completion and Ongoing Care

Following completion, we can recommend an appropriate future cleaning schedule where ongoing maintenance would be beneficial.
